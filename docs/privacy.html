FoCo Social privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

FoCo Social is a student-made app for '53 Commons (FoCo) at Dartmouth. It isn't run by Dartmouth College or Dartmouth Dining. There are no ads and no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Without an account

The menu, diet filters, and food alerts work without signing in. Your diet settings and the foods you follow are stored only on your phone. The menu comes from Dartmouth Dining's public menu.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your Dartmouth email address, used to sign in with an emailed code. Other students never see it.
  • Your name, class year, and profile photo if you add one.
  • What you add in the app: friends and groups, meal plans and replies, check-ins, plate photos and captions, upvotes, reports, and food requests.

Who can see what

  • Name, class year, profile picture: other signed-in Dartmouth students, so they can find you.
  • Check-ins (that you're at FoCo, which room, or when you'll arrive): only your friends. They expire on their own within three hours.
  • Meal plans: the friends, people, or group you post them to.
  • Group name and photo: people in the group.
  • Plate photos and captions: signed-in Dartmouth students. Photos are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses; anyone who has the exact address can open the image.
  • Food requests: Dartmouth Dining receives one email a day listing each requested food and how many students asked. It never includes names.
  • Blocking someone hides your posts, plans, and check-ins from each other.

Location

Only used if you turn on automatic check-in. Your phone checks whether you're inside FoCo during a meal. Your location never leaves your phone; friends only see that you're at FoCo.

Notifications

Food alerts are scheduled on your phone from the posted menu. Invite notifications come from the app checking for new invites. No notification content is sent to anyone else.

Where it's kept

Accounts, posts, and photos are stored with Supabase, a hosted database service. Sign-in codes and the daily Dining email are sent through Gmail.

Moderation

Posts reported by three people are hidden until reviewed. Accounts that break the community rules (no harassment, hate, nudity, or photos of people without their OK) are removed.

Deleting your data

Delete any post from its ••• menu. In the app, Me → Delete my account permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, plans, check-ins, friends, and requests. Requests already emailed to Dining stay in that email, which never had your name.
