FoCo Social privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

FoCo Social is a student-made app for '53 Commons (FoCo) at Dartmouth. It isn't run by Dartmouth College or Dartmouth Dining. There are no ads and no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Without an account

The menu, diet filters, and food alerts work without signing in. Your diet settings and the foods you follow are stored only on your phone. The menu comes from Dartmouth Dining's public menu.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your Dartmouth email address, used to sign in with an emailed code. Other students never see it.
  • Your name, class year, and profile photo if you add one.
  • What you add in the app: friends, close friends, and groups, meal plans and replies, check-ins, messages, reports, and food requests.
  • If you allow notifications, your phone's notification address (a push token from Apple) and your notification settings.

Who can see what

  • Name, class year, profile picture: other signed-in Dartmouth students, so they can find you.
  • Check-ins (that you're at FoCo, which side, or when you'll arrive): your friends, or only your close friends if you choose that. They expire on their own within three hours.
  • Meal plans: the friends, people, or group you post them to. Everyone invited to a plan can see who else is invited and how they answered.
  • Messages: the friend you message, the people in the group, or the people in the meal plan. They stay until you unsend them or delete your account.
  • Your close friends list: only you.
  • Group name and photo: people in the group and people invited to it.
  • Profile and group photos are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses; anyone who has the exact address can open the image.
  • Food requests: Dartmouth Dining receives one email a day listing each requested food and how many students asked. It never includes names.
  • Blocking someone hides your plans, check-ins, and messages from each other.

Location

Only used if you turn on automatic check-in. Your phone checks whether you're inside FoCo during a meal. Your location never leaves your phone; friends only see that you're at FoCo.

Notifications

Food alerts and plan reminders are scheduled on your phone. Notifications about friends (going soon, invites, messages) are sent by our server through Apple's push notification service, which delivers them to your phone. You choose which ones you get in Me → Notifications.

Where it's kept

Accounts, messages, and photos are stored with Supabase, a hosted database service. Notifications are delivered by Apple. Sign-in codes and the daily Dining email are sent through Gmail.

Moderation

Press and hold any message to report it; reported messages are reviewed. Accounts that break the community rules (no harassment, hate, nudity, or photos of people without their OK) are removed.

Deleting your data

Unsend any message by pressing and holding it. In the app, Me → Delete my account permanently removes your profile, photos, plans, check-ins, messages, friends, notification settings, and requests. Requests already emailed to Dining stay in that email, which never had your name.
