Foco Polo privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Foco Polo is an app for '53 Commons (Foco) at Dartmouth. There are no ads, nothing follows you across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold.

Without an account

The menu, diet filters, and food alerts work without signing in. Your diet settings and the foods you follow are stored only on your phone. The menu comes from Dartmouth Dining's public menu.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your Dartmouth email address, used to sign in with an emailed code. Other students never see it.
  • Your name, class year, and profile photo if you add one.
  • What you add in the app: friends, close friends, and groups, meal plans and replies, and check-ins.
  • Which features you used on which day (for example, that you opened the menu or posted going soon). No times, places, or anything you wrote. We use it only to see what's working, and it's deleted with your account.
  • If you allow notifications, your phone's notification address (a push token from Apple) and your notification settings.

Who can see what

  • Name, class year, profile picture: other signed-in Dartmouth students, so they can find you.
  • Check-ins (that you're at Foco, which side, or when you'll arrive): your friends, or only your close friends if you choose that. They expire on their own within three hours.
  • Meal plans: the friends, people, or group you post them to. Everyone invited to a plan can see who else is invited and how they answered.
  • Your close friends list: only you.
  • Group name and photo: people in the group and people invited to it.
  • Profile and group photos are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses; anyone who has the exact address can open the image.
  • Which features you used: only you (in the app) and the people who run Foco Polo.
  • Blocking someone hides your plans and check-ins from each other.

Location

Only used if you turn on automatic check-in. Your phone checks whether you're inside Foco during a meal. Your location never leaves your phone; friends only see that you're at Foco.

Notifications

Food alerts and plan reminders are scheduled on your phone. Notifications about friends (going soon, invites, plan changes) are sent by our server through Apple's push notification service, which delivers them to your phone. You choose which ones you get in Me → Notifications.

Where it's kept

Accounts, plans, and photos are stored with Supabase, a hosted database service. Notifications are delivered by Apple. Sign-in codes are sent through Gmail.

Moderation

Press and hold anyone's name to report or block them; reports come to us by email and are reviewed. Accounts that break the community rules (no harassment, hate, nudity, or photos of people without their OK) are removed.

Deleting your data

In the app, Me → Delete my account permanently removes your profile, photos, plans, check-ins, friends, notification settings, and feature counts, plus anything from earlier test versions (messages and food requests). Food requests already emailed then stay in that email, which never had your name.
